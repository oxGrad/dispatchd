What the service is

dispatchd automates a team's daily standup ritual inside one Discord server: it prompts team members for their todos and progress, mirrors those submissions into a daily thread, sends reminders, and lets the team lead pull a periodic recap. It is a convenience tool, not a system of record, and is intended for a small, known group of team members that the operator configures.
